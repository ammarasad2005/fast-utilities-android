# Privacy Policy — FAST Utilities

_Last updated: 30 August 2026_

FAST Utilities **does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information**.
There are no user accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

## What the app does with data

- **Schedule data** — the app downloads publicly published university timetable and
  exam-schedule data from the project's schedule backend purely to display it to you.
- **On-device storage** — your selections (timetable tag, exam tag, theme) are stored
  only on your device and never leave it.
- **Updates** — the app checks GitHub Releases for new versions and, only with your
  confirmation, downloads the update APK.

## Permissions used

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet | Fetch schedule data and app updates |
| Notifications | Optional class and exam reminders (can be denied) |
| Install unknown apps | Self-update, only after you confirm each update |
